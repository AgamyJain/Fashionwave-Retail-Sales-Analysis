## Project Abstract

FashionWave is a retail analytics case study focused on understanding sales performance across stores, products, and time periods. The project uses Excel to examine retail sales patterns and support business decisions related to weekend sales, product performance, and discount strategies.

The analysis is intended to help management understand differences between weekend and weekday sales, identify high- and low-performing products or stores, and evaluate how discounting relates to sales and profitability measures.

The project combines spreadsheet-based analysis, KPI evaluation, and business interpretation to develop actionable recommendations. Findings and recommendations should be interpreted within the scope of the supplied dataset and the assumptions documented in the accompanying analysis.

## Business Scenario

FashionWave is presented as a mid-sized retail chain operating 50 stores across India. Management wants to understand why weekend sales have remained stagnant while weekday sales have been relatively consistent.

The business needs evidence-based recommendations to improve weekend performance and make more informed decisions about products, stores, and discounts.

## Business Objectives

- Investigate weekend sales performance against weekday sales.
- Identify products and categories contributing to sales.
- Examine store-level performance.
- Assess discount-related patterns and their relationship with profitability.
- Recommend practical strategies for improving retail performance.

## Business Questions

1. Which product categories generate the highest sales?
2. How do weekend sales compare with weekday sales?
3. Which stores perform relatively well or poorly?
4. How are discounts associated with sales and profit margins?
5. What actions could improve weekend sales and product performance?

## Key Findings

The project investigates differences in weekend and weekday sales, product performance, store-level results, and discount-related patterns.

Consult the Excel workbook, report, and presentation for the final numerical findings. Recommendations should be based on the results calculated in those deliverables.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate the stores and product categories contributing most to weekend sales.
- Review underperforming products and stores before changing inventory or promotional strategies.
- Evaluate discounts using both sales and profit-related measures.
- Test targeted weekend promotions and compare results against a documented baseline.
- Monitor performance over time to determine whether recommended actions improve results.
